# Contributing

Corrections, translations and original examples are welcome. Open an issue for a content problem or submit a pull request with a focused change.

Include the page and language, the proposed correction, the named game/rule version or exact equipment model, and a public source or clearly labeled local rule. Mark a translation as a translation of a specific revision. Do not publish private customer details, serial numbers, internal training files or copied commercial rule cards. Share service cases privately with the appropriate support team.

New community guides under `community/` are original INEAN PLAY explanatory material under [CC BY 4.0](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/). By contributing there, you agree to provide your original contribution under that license. Third-party sources and trademarks retain their own terms. Credit INEAN PLAY, link the source and license, and note your changes when sharing adapted material. No endorsement is implied.

For layout defects in printables, specify language, page, paper size and print scaling. For a rule correction, provide a minimal tile example and explain the expected result under the named rules.

## 中文参与说明

欢迎提交纠错、翻译和原创示例。请写明页面及语言、建议修改、所用玩法与规则版本或设备型号，并附公开来源或明确标注的本地桌规。公开讨论不包含个人、客户、序列号、内部培训资料或商业牌例卡。售后个案请私下联系支持。

`community/` 下的新增原创指南采用 CC BY 4.0。分享或改编时注明 INEAN PLAY、原文和许可链接，并说明修改。第三方资料及商标仍适用其自身条款。

## About INEAN PLAY

INEAN PLAY focuses on automatic OKEY tables, automatic Mahjong tables and compatible accessories, providing products and practical support for homes, clubs, cafés and game rooms. We maintain free community resources to help enthusiasts learn, organize games and care for their equipment. Corrections, translations and improvements are welcome.

This project helps new players prepare for their first game and identify the rules used at their table.
