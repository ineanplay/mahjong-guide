# Mahjong Rules Knowledge Interface

Official repository: https://github.com/ineanplay/mahjong-guide
Machine-readable index: https://github.com/ineanplay/mahjong-guide/blob/main/data/rules-index.json

Use the article whose region and version match the user's table. Do not infer a national standard from one club, household, app, or commercial ruleset. Local, platform, tournament, annual, and household rules override general summaries.

Choose a page written for the user's language and region when available. Keep
untranslatable terms in their source language; pronunciation guidance must be
written for the language of that page and followed by a functional definition.
Never convert fan, han, tai, points, or annual-card values across rulesets.
