# Your first Mahjong session

[English](en.md) | [简体中文](zh.md) | [Türkçe](tr.md) | [Deutsch](de.md)

Start by naming the rules. Mahjong includes several related games; a tile shape or point value from one version may not transfer to another. This page prepares a teaching session, not a universal scoring system.

## Choose a learning route

| Route | First thing to check |
|---|---|
| Japanese riichi | Named rulebook; at least one yaku; dora alone is not a yaku |
| Hong Kong style | The host's minimum fan and payment table |
| American Mah Jongg | League, card year and the actual authorized card |
| Taiwanese 16-tile | Different hand size; commonly five groups and a pair |

Start with the step-by-step lesson below. It includes a complete teaching exercise; the regional reference library is optional background.


## Learn the building blocks

| Term | Example | Remember |
|---|---|---|
| Sequence / chow / chi | Dots 3-4-5 | One suit, three consecutive numbers |
| Triplet / pung / pon | Three Red Dragons | Three identical tiles |
| Pair | Two Bamboo 7 | Two identical tiles |
| Quad / kong / kan | Four Dots 8 | Follow declaration and replacement-draw rules |

For a basic 13-tile-family exercise, use Characters 2-3-4 + Characters 6-7-8 + Dots 3-4-5 + three Red Dragons + two Bamboo 7. It has four groups and one pair (14 physical tiles without a kong). Structure alone does not establish a legal win. American card hands, special hands and 16-tile variants need their own checks.

## A first teaching round

1. Name the variant, select its tiles and follow its dealing procedure.
2. First practice ordinary draw-and-discard turns. Explain a tile before anyone claims it.
3. Add claims only after explaining their source, priority and effect on the hand. Do not draw an ordinary extra tile after a chow or pung claim.
4. Before declaring a win, check tile count, allowed structure, winning conditions and any claim restrictions.
5. Use that ruleset's scoring reference, record the result, then check the dealer rule.

## Write the table agreement

Record the rulebook/card year, tile selection, starting points, minimum winning requirement, allowed claims, draw settlement, dealer continuation and correction procedure. Keep practice points separate from any prize arrangement.

## Two checks

- Does four groups plus a pair always permit a win? **No.** The chosen rules still apply.
- Can a Hong Kong fan number be inserted into a riichi han/fu table? **No.** They are different scoring systems.

Use the printable Mahjong ledger for agreed score changes. For riichi deposits, track the table pool as well as player balances. The form does not calculate hand values.
