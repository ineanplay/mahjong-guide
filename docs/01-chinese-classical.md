# Chinese Classical Mahjong: Beginner Rules

> **Scope:** China / historical international play
> **Version:** Rule family; scoring must be agreed
> **Summary:** A traditional four-player foundation for learning melds, honors, flowers, and hand scoring.

## Equipment and goal

Four players commonly use 144 tiles: 108 suited tiles, 28 Winds and Dragons,
and eight Flowers and Seasons. Some tables omit bonus tiles. A normal win is
four sets and a pair.

## Setup and play

Build a wall, determine East, and deal 13 tiles to each player; East starts with
14 and discards first. On a turn, draw from the wall and discard. A sequence is
normally claimed only from the player on the left. A triplet, kong, or winning
tile may usually be claimed from any player, subject to claim priority.

Exposed sets remain face up. A kong needs a replacement draw. Flowers and
Seasons are exposed and replaced immediately when the chosen rule set uses
them.

## Scoring

There is no single worldwide Chinese Classical scoring table. Traditional
tables may count points, doubles, limits, dealer multipliers, and payment from
the discarder differently. Agree on the scoring sheet and minimum hand before
dealing.

## Beginner strategy

Keep connected suited tiles and one useful pair. Honors are valuable when they
match the seat wind, round wind, or a Dragon, but isolated honors become harder
to use after several copies are discarded. Do not expose a set unless the speed
or value gained is worth revealing your plan.
