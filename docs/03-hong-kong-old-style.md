# Hong Kong Old Style Mahjong: Beginner Rules

> **Scope:** Hong Kong and Cantonese communities
> **Version:** Common framework; fan tables vary
> **Summary:** A fast four-player style built around fan, Flowers, honors, and straightforward hand patterns.

## Equipment and hand

Hong Kong Old Style usually uses four players and a 144-tile set. A normal win
is four sets and a pair. Flowers and Seasons are exposed and replaced; their
value depends on the table rules.

## Turn and claims

East discards first. Players draw and discard counter-clockwise. A chow is
normally called only from the player on the left; pungs, kongs, and wins can be
claimed more broadly. Exposed calls speed the hand but reduce concealment.

## Fan and settlement

Winning patterns are valued in fan. Common tables use a minimum such as three
fan, but one-fan and other house systems also exist. Limit hands, Flower
payments, self-draw bonuses, dealer multipliers, and discard responsibility vary
considerably.

Before playing, agree on the minimum fan, limit value, Flower rules, dealer
continuation, and whether several players may win from one discard.

## Beginner strategy

Dragon pungs, seat or round winds, all-pungs shapes, one-suit plans, and concealed
hands are common routes to value. Count your fan before declaring a win. A
complete shape below the table minimum is not a legal win.
