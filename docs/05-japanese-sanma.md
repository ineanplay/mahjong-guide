# Japanese Three-Player Mahjong (Sanma) Beginner Guide

> **Scope:** Japan / online platforms
> **Version:** Variant family; platform rules vary
> **Summary:** A three-player riichi variant with a reduced tile set and important rule differences.

## What changes from four-player riichi

Sanma normally has three players and removes most Character tiles from 2 through
8, leaving 108 tiles. Chii is often unavailable, while pon, kan, ron, and tsumo
remain. The North tile may be treated as a removable bonus tile (nuki-dora) in
many systems, but not all.

## Scoring differences

Payments must be adjusted because only three players contribute. Tsumo loss,
dealer payments, honba, red fives, North handling, and whether a hand may use the
remaining 1 and 9 Characters differ by platform.

## Beginner strategy

Hands develop faster because the wall is smaller and middle Character tiles are
absent. Flushes, honors, terminals, and high-value direct attacks appear more
often. Defense still matters: riichi, furiten, yaku, and dora concepts remain,
but safe-tile reading uses a different tile population.

## Before playing

Confirm nuki-dora, red tiles, tsumo payment, open tanyao, multiple ron, abortive
draws, and local yakuman. Use the room or tournament rule page as the authority.
