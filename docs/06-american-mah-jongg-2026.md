# American Mah Jongg 2026: Beginner Guide

> **Scope:** United States
> **Version:** 2026 NMJL card required
> **Summary:** Learn the stable American Mah Jongg procedure while using the official 2026 card for current hands and values.

## 2026 version notice

The National Mah Jongg League publishes a new card each year. This page applies
to the 2026 card. The permitted hands, groupings, concealed status, and values
must be read from an authentic 2026 card. This guide does not reproduce the
copyrighted card and should not be used with a 2025 or earlier card.

## Equipment and goal

American Mah Jongg commonly uses 152 tiles, including eight Jokers, plus racks
and dice. The goal is to assemble one exact hand printed on the current card.
Unlike many Asian variants, a generic four-sets-and-a-pair shape is not enough.

## Deal and Charleston

Players receive 13 tiles and East begins with 14. Before normal play, players
perform the Charleston passing sequence. The required and optional passes,
courtesy pass, and stopping conditions must follow the current official rules
and the table agreement.

## Drawing, calling, and Jokers

On each turn, draw and discard. A discarded tile may be called only when it
completes an allowed exposed grouping or the declared Mah Jongg, subject to the
current rules. Jokers generally substitute in eligible groups of identical
tiles, not singles or pairs. An exposed Joker may be exchanged for its matching
natural tile under the official procedure.

## Beginner strategy

Choose two or three related card hands after the deal, then narrow the plan as
the Charleston reveals strengths and shortages. Check whether the chosen line
is concealed before calling. Keep the card visible and verify every symbol,
color requirement, grouping, and year-specific value before declaring.
