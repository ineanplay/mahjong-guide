# Singapore Mahjong: Beginner Rules

> **Scope:** Singapore
> **Version:** Common framework; animal and payout rules vary
> **Summary:** A four-player style often using Flowers, Seasons, Animals, and local limit-hand rules.

## Tile set and goal

Singapore Mahjong commonly uses the standard suited and honor tiles, Flowers
and Seasons, plus four Animal tiles, often for 148 tiles in total. Some sets and
tables add or remove special tiles. A normal win remains four sets and a pair.

## Special tiles

Flowers, Seasons, and Animals are normally exposed and replaced. Matching a
bonus tile to a seat, collecting groups of bonuses, or revealing particular
Animals may produce immediate or end-of-hand payments. These rules are highly
table-specific.

## Play and scoring

Normal draw, discard, chow, pung, kong, and win procedures apply. Scoring often
uses fan or tai with a table limit. Chicken hand eligibility, minimum fan,
shooter responsibility, self-draw, dealer continuation, and special hands must
be agreed before play.

## Beginner strategy

Replace bonus tiles immediately and keep them visible. Separate instant bonus
payments from hand-value scoring. Build a legal base hand first, then evaluate
whether Dragons, Winds, all-pungs, or suit concentration can reach the table
minimum.
