# Malaysian Three-Player Mahjong: Beginner Guide

> **Scope:** Malaysia
> **Version:** Regional family; tile inventory must be agreed
> **Summary:** An introduction to fast Malaysian three-player tables with reduced tiles and local bonus rules.

## No single national ruleset

Malaysian three-player Mahjong is a family of local games rather than one fixed
standard. Many tables use a reduced tile set, emphasize one suited family and
honors, and include Flowers, Animals, or Jokers. Tile inventory and Joker powers
must be stated before the deal.

## Common flow

Three players draw and discard while forming a legal local hand. Calls, bonus
replacement tiles, instant payments, Joker substitution, and limit hands depend
on the regional or household rules. A published table sheet is strongly
recommended.

## Beginner checklist

- Count the exact tiles in play.
- Identify every special tile and whether it is wild, a bonus, or both.
- Confirm the winning hand size and minimum value.
- Confirm whether sequences are allowed and from whom they may be called.
- Separate immediate bonus payments from the final hand settlement.

Do not assume Singapore, Hong Kong, or online three-player rules apply just
because the tile set looks similar.
