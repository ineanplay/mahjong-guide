# Vietnamese Mahjong: Beginner Guide

> **Scope:** Vietnam
> **Version:** Regional family; special-tile powers vary
> **Summary:** A Mahjong family often using extra special tiles beyond the standard 144-tile set.

## Equipment

Vietnamese Mahjong sets commonly expand the standard 144 tiles with additional
special tiles, often producing a 160-tile set. Names, artwork, replacement
rules, and wild powers vary by region and manufacturer.

## Core play

The underlying game generally follows four-player draw-and-discard Mahjong with
sets, a pair, calls, kongs, and bonus replacement tiles. Special tiles may act
as Jokers, bonuses, or restricted substitutes, so their exact function must be
learned from the table sheet.

## Beginner checklist

Lay out and identify all special tiles before shuffling. Confirm which tiles
are Flowers, which are wild, whether a wild can form a pair, and whether a hand
needs a minimum value. Record the agreed scoring and limit rules.

## Strategy

Do not use a special tile immediately just because it is flexible. Its value may
depend on concealment, substitution limits, or a later combination. First learn
the stable standard-tile structure, then add the local special-tile rules.
