# Filipino Mahjong: Beginner Guide

> **Scope:** Philippines
> **Version:** Regional and household rules vary
> **Summary:** An introduction to Filipino Mahjong tables, which often use 16-tile hands and local bonus payments.

## Rule-family notice

Filipino Mahjong is not governed by one universal national rulebook. Many games
use 16-tile hands, requiring five sets and a pair, while calls, Flowers, special
patterns, and payment events differ by community.

## Common flow

Players draw and discard, claim permitted sets, replace bonus tiles, and form
the agreed winning structure. Some tables make immediate payments for events
such as special Flowers, kongs, or particular starting combinations.

## Before playing

Confirm the hand size, tile inventory, chow direction, minimum hand, instant
payments, dealer continuation, and the names and definitions of local special
hands. Use a written score sheet because similarly named patterns may have
different values.

## Beginner strategy

Count five sets before declaring a 16-tile win. Keep immediate payments separate
from final settlement and ask the table to explain special local terms before
the first deal.
