# Korean Mahjong: Beginner Guide

> **Scope:** Korea
> **Version:** Regional rules vary; confirm the scoring sheet
> **Summary:** A concise orientation to Korean Mahjong and its regional differences from Chinese and Japanese rules.

## Rule-family notice

Korean Mahjong is played in several local forms and does not have one globally
recognized scoring standard. Tile inventory, use of Flowers, sequence calls,
minimum value, and Japanese-influenced concepts can vary between groups.

## Stable learning approach

Begin with the standard suited and honor tiles and the four-sets-and-a-pair
structure. Learn the table's draw direction, call priority, kong replacement,
and win declaration. Then add the local scoring patterns.

## Questions for the host

- Are Flowers used?
- Is a chow allowed, and from which player?
- Is there a minimum score?
- Are ready declarations, dora, or furiten used?
- How are self-draw, dealer, and draws settled?

## Beginner strategy

Do not assume Japanese riichi rules apply because Korean terminology or media
may borrow Japanese concepts. Use the actual table sheet, keep a flexible base
hand, and verify legality before declaring.
