# Chinese Regional Mahjong: Comparison and Table-Rule Checklist

> **Scope:** Mainland China regional overview
> **Version:** Orientation, not a single national ruleset
> **Summary:** Compare major regional rule families without treating one app, club, or household as the authority for a province.

## Scope and reliability

This page describes a regional rule family, not a government or nationwide standard. A city, province, or country label can cover several club, household, and platform variants.

## Commonly documented features

- Chinese regional games differ in tile inventory, winning threshold, special opening events, wild tiles, continuing play after a win, and payment responsibility.
- Sichuan tables often remove honors; Wuhan and Nanchang families commonly feature wild-tile mechanisms; Changsha is known for suited-tile play and opening-pattern checks; coastal variants may retain Flowers or use local bonus tiles.
- A city name identifies a rule family, not a complete score sheet. Platforms frequently publish different options under the same name.

## Confirm before the first deal

- Exact tile inventory
- Wild-tile selection and restrictions
- Minimum winning condition
- Multiple-win and dealer rules
- Scoring and special starting events

## Beginner approach

Start with the ordinary draw-and-discard rhythm and four-sets-and-a-pair structure unless the host states otherwise. Ask for one legal winning example and one complete settlement example. Keep local terms beside their definitions, and never declare a win solely because a different app or regional guide accepts the same tile shape.
