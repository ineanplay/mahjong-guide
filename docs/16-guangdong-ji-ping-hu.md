# Guangdong Ji Ping Hu Mahjong: Beginner Orientation

> **Scope:** Guangdong, China
> **Version:** Local rule family; fan and payment tables vary
> **Summary:** Learn the common shape of Guangdong Ji Ping Hu and the options that must be agreed before play.

## Scope and reliability

This page describes a regional rule family, not a government or nationwide standard. A city, province, or country label can cover several club, household, and platform variants.

## Commonly documented features

- Ji Ping Hu is a Cantonese regional family related to, but not identical with, Hong Kong Old Style Mahjong.
- Four sets and a pair remain the normal winning structure. Tables commonly distinguish a low-value basic win from pattern-bearing hands, while Flowers, self-draw, dealer, kongs, and responsibility payments may alter settlement.
- Names such as Chicken Hand, All Pungs, Pure One Suit, and Mixed One Suit are widely recognizable, but their eligibility and fan values are not universal.

## Confirm before the first deal

- Minimum fan
- Flower and Season treatment
- Basic-win eligibility
- Discard responsibility
- Dealer continuation and limit hands

## Beginner approach

Start with the ordinary draw-and-discard rhythm and four-sets-and-a-pair structure unless the host states otherwise. Ask for one legal winning example and one complete settlement example. Keep local terms beside their definitions, and never declare a win solely because a different app or regional guide accepts the same tile shape.
