# Shanghai Mahjong: Beginner Orientation

> **Scope:** Shanghai, China
> **Version:** Local variants differ in Flowers and scoring
> **Summary:** Understand the shared Mahjong core and the local Flower, pattern, and settlement questions for Shanghai tables.

## Scope and reliability

This page describes a regional rule family, not a government or nationwide standard. A city, province, or country label can cover several club, household, and platform variants.

## Commonly documented features

- Shanghai-named rules generally retain suited tiles and honors, with many tables also using Flowers as replacements or bonuses.
- The legal winning structure is often conventional, but local pattern values, minimum thresholds, Flower effects, kong payments, and limit handling vary substantially.
- Some online implementations simplify or redefine the local score table, so platform rules must be treated as platform-specific.

## Confirm before the first deal

- Flower inventory and replacement
- Minimum score
- Pattern definitions
- Kong payments
- Dealer and draw rules

## Beginner approach

Start with the ordinary draw-and-discard rhythm and four-sets-and-a-pair structure unless the host states otherwise. Ask for one legal winning example and one complete settlement example. Keep local terms beside their definitions, and never declare a win solely because a different app or regional guide accepts the same tile shape.
