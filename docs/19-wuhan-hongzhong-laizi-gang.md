# Wuhan Hongzhong Laizi Gang Mahjong: Beginner Guide

> **Scope:** Wuhan and Hubei communities, China
> **Version:** Common rule family; laizi and scoring details vary
> **Summary:** Learn the wild-tile concept, red-dragon mechanism, and the restrictions that must be checked at a Wuhan table.

## Scope and reliability

This page describes a regional rule family, not a government or nationwide standard. A city, province, or country label can cover several club, household, and platform variants.

## Commonly documented features

- Wuhan Hongzhong Laizi Gang is associated with a laizi, or wild tile, that can substitute within legal combinations under local restrictions.
- The Red Dragon has a special relationship to the wild-tile system in commonly described versions, but selection, replacement, kong treatment, and winning restrictions are table-specific.
- A hand that is structurally complete may still be illegal if it misses the local opening or scoring requirement, or uses the laizi in a prohibited way.

## Confirm before the first deal

- How the laizi is chosen
- What Red Dragons do
- Whether laizi may form a pair or special hand
- Minimum win requirement
- Kong and discard payments

## Beginner approach

Start with the ordinary draw-and-discard rhythm and four-sets-and-a-pair structure unless the host states otherwise. Ask for one legal winning example and one complete settlement example. Keep local terms beside their definitions, and never declare a win solely because a different app or regional guide accepts the same tile shape.
