# Changsha Mahjong: Beginner Guide

> **Scope:** Changsha and Hunan communities, China
> **Version:** Common features documented; exact scoring is table-specific
> **Summary:** Learn the 108-tile base, opening-pattern checks, and bird-draw settlement commonly associated with Changsha Mahjong.

## Scope and reliability

This page describes a regional rule family, not a government or nationwide standard. A city, province, or country label can cover several club, household, and platform variants.

## Commonly documented features

- Common Changsha rules use the 108 suited tiles and omit Winds and Dragons.
- Tables may inspect special starting patterns commonly translated or romanized as Four Happiness, Banban Hu, Liuliushun, and Missing One Suit.
- Bird drawing after a win is a common settlement feature, but the number of birds, seat mapping, scoring, and treatment after a draw differ among tables and platforms.

## Confirm before the first deal

- Recognized starting patterns
- Basic winning requirement
- Bird count and mapping
- Multiple wins
- Dealer continuation

## Beginner approach

Start with the ordinary draw-and-discard rhythm and four-sets-and-a-pair structure unless the host states otherwise. Ask for one legal winning example and one complete settlement example. Keep local terms beside their definitions, and never declare a win solely because a different app or regional guide accepts the same tile shape.
