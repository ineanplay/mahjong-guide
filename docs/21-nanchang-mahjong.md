# Nanchang Mahjong: Beginner Orientation

> **Scope:** Nanchang and Jiangxi communities, China
> **Version:** Local wild-tile family; confirm all laizi rules
> **Summary:** A cautious introduction to Nanchang-style wild tiles, local bonuses, and pre-game decisions.

## Scope and reliability

This page describes a regional rule family, not a government or nationwide standard. A city, province, or country label can cover several club, household, and platform variants.

## Commonly documented features

- Nanchang Mahjong is commonly described with a local wild-tile mechanism and bonus concepts that change both hand construction and settlement.
- The indicator procedure, which tiles become wild, whether a wild tile may be used naturally, and restrictions on a wild-assisted win differ between implementations.
- Local terminology is not consistently translated, so the host's examples are more reliable than a bare pattern name.

## Confirm before the first deal

- Wild-tile indicator
- Wild-use restrictions
- Bonus tiles or events
- Legal win threshold
- Settlement examples

## Beginner approach

Start with the ordinary draw-and-discard rhythm and four-sets-and-a-pair structure unless the host states otherwise. Ask for one legal winning example and one complete settlement example. Keep local terms beside their definitions, and never declare a win solely because a different app or regional guide accepts the same tile shape.
