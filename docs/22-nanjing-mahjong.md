# Nanjing Mahjong: Beginner Orientation

> **Scope:** Nanjing and Jiangsu communities, China
> **Version:** Local rule family; special-tile rules vary
> **Summary:** Identify Nanjing table options involving Flowers, special tiles, winning requirements, and settlement.

## Scope and reliability

This page describes a regional rule family, not a government or nationwide standard. A city, province, or country label can cover several club, household, and platform variants.

## Commonly documented features

- Nanjing-named games retain the Mahjong draw-and-discard core but may use local Flower or special-tile procedures.
- Tables differ on tile inventory, replacement draws, minimum winning value, special combinations, and how exposed kongs are paid.
- Do not assume Shanghai or other Jiangsu rules apply merely because the equipment looks similar.

## Confirm before the first deal

- Complete tile list
- Replacement procedure
- Minimum hand
- Special combinations
- Kong and dealer payments

## Beginner approach

Start with the ordinary draw-and-discard rhythm and four-sets-and-a-pair structure unless the host states otherwise. Ask for one legal winning example and one complete settlement example. Keep local terms beside their definitions, and never declare a win solely because a different app or regional guide accepts the same tile shape.
