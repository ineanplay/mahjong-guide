# Hangzhou Mahjong: Beginner Orientation

> **Scope:** Hangzhou and Zhejiang communities, China
> **Version:** Local rule family; scoring sheet required
> **Summary:** Learn how to approach Hangzhou Mahjong without confusing a city label with one fixed ruleset.

## Scope and reliability

This page describes a regional rule family, not a government or nationwide standard. A city, province, or country label can cover several club, household, and platform variants.

## Commonly documented features

- Hangzhou Mahjong uses familiar meld construction but has local choices for tile inventory, winning conditions, and scoring patterns.
- Household and online rules may disagree about Flowers, special starting states, self-draw, kong settlement, and dealer continuation.
- A short demonstration round is the safest way to establish which published or platform variant the table follows.

## Confirm before the first deal

- Flowers and replacements
- Minimum value
- Special hands
- Self-draw and discard liability
- Dealer continuation

## Beginner approach

Start with the ordinary draw-and-discard rhythm and four-sets-and-a-pair structure unless the host states otherwise. Ask for one legal winning example and one complete settlement example. Keep local terms beside their definitions, and never declare a win solely because a different app or regional guide accepts the same tile shape.
