# Fujian Jin Mahjong: Beginner Orientation

> **Scope:** Fujian communities, China
> **Version:** Multiple local Jin systems; no province-wide standard
> **Summary:** Understand the local Jin or gold-tile idea and why its selection and use must be confirmed for each table.

## Scope and reliability

This page describes a regional rule family, not a government or nationwide standard. A city, province, or country label can cover several club, household, and platform variants.

## Commonly documented features

- Several Fujian Mahjong families use a tile commonly called Jin, often translated as gold, with wild or bonus functions.
- How Jin is selected, whether one or more Jin exist, natural versus substitute use, and restrictions on winning with Jin differ by city and ruleset.
- Fuzhou, Xiamen, Quanzhou, and other community rules should not be collapsed into one province-wide scoring system.

## Confirm before the first deal

- City or community ruleset
- Jin selection
- Jin substitution limits
- Flower and honor inventory
- Win and payment rules

## Beginner approach

Start with the ordinary draw-and-discard rhythm and four-sets-and-a-pair structure unless the host states otherwise. Ask for one legal winning example and one complete settlement example. Keep local terms beside their definitions, and never declare a win solely because a different app or regional guide accepts the same tile shape.
