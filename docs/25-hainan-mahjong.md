# Hainan Mahjong: Beginner Orientation

> **Scope:** Hainan communities, China
> **Version:** Local variations; verify special tiles and scoring
> **Summary:** A table-first guide to Hainan Mahjong equipment, local bonuses, and settlement choices.

## Scope and reliability

This page describes a regional rule family, not a government or nationwide standard. A city, province, or country label can cover several club, household, and platform variants.

## Commonly documented features

- Hainan Mahjong belongs to the broad southern Chinese family but local tables may use distinct bonus, Flower, or winning rules.
- The standard four-sets-and-a-pair concept is a useful starting point, not a complete statement of legality or score.
- Community, club, and online descriptions may represent different local traditions.

## Confirm before the first deal

- Tile inventory
- Bonus and Flower tiles
- Winning threshold
- Special hands
- Dealer, draw, and payment rules

## Beginner approach

Start with the ordinary draw-and-discard rhythm and four-sets-and-a-pair structure unless the host states otherwise. Ask for one legal winning example and one complete settlement example. Keep local terms beside their definitions, and never declare a win solely because a different app or regional guide accepts the same tile shape.
