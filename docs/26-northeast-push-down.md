# Northeast Push-Down Mahjong: Beginner Orientation

> **Scope:** Northeast China
> **Version:** Broad local family; city rules differ
> **Summary:** Learn the fast, simplified character of Push-Down Mahjong and the exact options a host must specify.

## Scope and reliability

This page describes a regional rule family, not a government or nationwide standard. A city, province, or country label can cover several club, household, and platform variants.

## Commonly documented features

- Push-Down Mahjong is a broad name used for comparatively direct regional games in Northeast China.
- A conventional winning shape and accessible scoring are common themes, while honors, Flowers, allowed calls, required patterns, and special payments vary by city.
- Rules labeled Harbin, Shenyang, Changchun, or Northeast should be treated as related but not interchangeable.

## Confirm before the first deal

- Named city variant
- Tiles removed or retained
- Minimum requirement
- Allowed calls
- Dealer and special payments

## Beginner approach

Start with the ordinary draw-and-discard rhythm and four-sets-and-a-pair structure unless the host states otherwise. Ask for one legal winning example and one complete settlement example. Keep local terms beside their definitions, and never declare a win solely because a different app or regional guide accepts the same tile shape.
