# Malaysian Four-Player Mahjong: Beginner Orientation

> **Scope:** Malaysia
> **Version:** Community rules vary by language group and location
> **Summary:** Distinguish Malaysian four-player play from the better-known local three-player family.

## Scope and reliability

This page describes a regional rule family, not a government or nationwide standard. A city, province, or country label can cover several club, household, and platform variants.

## Commonly documented features

- Malaysian four-player Mahjong commonly draws on southern Chinese rule families while adding locally recognized bonus tiles and payments.
- It must not be confused with Malaysian three-player Mahjong, which can use a reduced tile inventory and substantially different bonus mechanics.
- Animal tiles, Flowers, Seasons, instant payments, minimum fan, and limit hands vary among communities.

## Confirm before the first deal

- Four-player ruleset name
- Animal and bonus tiles
- Minimum fan
- Instant payments
- Limit and dealer rules

## Beginner approach

Start with the ordinary draw-and-discard rhythm and four-sets-and-a-pair structure unless the host states otherwise. Ask for one legal winning example and one complete settlement example. Keep local terms beside their definitions, and never declare a win solely because a different app or regional guide accepts the same tile shape.
