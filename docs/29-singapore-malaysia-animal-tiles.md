# Singapore and Malaysia Mahjong Animal Tiles Guide

> **Scope:** Singapore and Malaysia
> **Version:** Tile guide; effects and payments are table-specific
> **Summary:** Identify Cat, Rat, Rooster, Centipede, and other local bonus tiles without assuming universal scoring.

## Scope and reliability

This page describes a regional rule family, not a government or nationwide standard. A city, province, or country label can cover several club, household, and platform variants.

## Commonly documented features

- Some Singaporean and Malaysian sets include Animal tiles in addition to Flowers and Seasons.
- Commonly encountered pairs include Cat and Rat, and Rooster and Centipede. They are bonus tiles rather than suited tiles used to form ordinary sequences.
- Capture relationships, replacement draws, immediate payments, seat matching, and interactions with other bonus tiles depend on the chosen rules.

## Confirm before the first deal

- Which Animal tiles are present
- When they are exposed
- Replacement draws
- Capture or pairing effects
- Immediate and final payments

## Beginner approach

Start with the ordinary draw-and-discard rhythm and four-sets-and-a-pair structure unless the host states otherwise. Ask for one legal winning example and one complete settlement example. Keep local terms beside their definitions, and never declare a win solely because a different app or regional guide accepts the same tile shape.
