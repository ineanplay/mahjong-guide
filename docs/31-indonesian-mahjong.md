# Mahjong in Indonesia: Community Rules Orientation

> **Scope:** Indonesia
> **Version:** Community and club rules; no single national standard
> **Summary:** A practical checklist for identifying the Chinese-Indonesian or imported Mahjong system at a local table.

## Scope and reliability

This page describes a regional rule family, not a government or nationwide standard. A city, province, or country label can cover several club, household, and platform variants.

## Commonly documented features

- Indonesian Mahjong practice reflects diverse Chinese-Indonesian communities and imported modern rules rather than one national ruleset.
- Four-player Chinese-family games are a reasonable starting expectation, but Flowers, fan, local terminology, and settlement can differ.
- Online platform labels and informal translations should not be treated as authority for all Indonesian players.

## Confirm before the first deal

- Community or club rules
- Tile and Flower inventory
- Minimum fan
- Local pattern names
- Payments and dealer continuation

## Beginner approach

Start with the ordinary draw-and-discard rhythm and four-sets-and-a-pair structure unless the host states otherwise. Ask for one legal winning example and one complete settlement example. Keep local terms beside their definitions, and never declare a win solely because a different app or regional guide accepts the same tile shape.
