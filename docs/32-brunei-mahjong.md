# Mahjong in Brunei: Community Rules Orientation

> **Scope:** Brunei
> **Version:** Community rules; no verified unified national code
> **Summary:** Use equipment and table questions to identify the Malaysian, Singaporean, or Chinese-family rules in play.

## Scope and reliability

This page describes a regional rule family, not a government or nationwide standard. A city, province, or country label can cover several club, household, and platform variants.

## Commonly documented features

- Brunei tables may share features with neighboring Malaysian, Singaporean, and southern Chinese community rules.
- That regional relationship does not establish one Brunei-wide tile inventory or score table.
- Animal tiles, Flowers, minimum fan, and instant payments are especially important to confirm before the wall is built.

## Confirm before the first deal

- Named source rules
- Animal and Flower tiles
- Minimum fan
- Instant payments
- Dealer and draw settlement

## Beginner approach

Start with the ordinary draw-and-discard rhythm and four-sets-and-a-pair structure unless the host states otherwise. Ask for one legal winning example and one complete settlement example. Keep local terms beside their definitions, and never declare a win solely because a different app or regional guide accepts the same tile shape.
